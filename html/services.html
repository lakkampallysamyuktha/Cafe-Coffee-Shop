The Brewing Process

How a cup
gets made.

Five steps from bean to bar. None of them are shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Weigh

    Every dose is measured to the gram. Consistency starts before the grind.

  2. 02

    Grind

    Burr ground fresh for every cup. Re-dialled twice daily as humidity shifts.

  3. 03

    Extract

    Pressure, time and temperature are dialled for sweetness and clarity.

  4. 04

    Texture

    Milk steamed to Stackly. Any hotter and the sweetness burns off.

  5. 05

    Serve

    Straight to your table, at the temperature it was made to be drunk.
