Mission & Vision

Two ideas
we keep returning to.

01

Mission

Make coffee that is worth slowing down for, and a room that makes slowing down easy.

  • Roast in-house, in small batches
  • Pay producers properly
  • Never rush a pour
02

Vision

Become the kind of cafe that outlives its founders — a quiet institution for the neighborhood it sits in.

  • Train the next generation
  • Stay independent
  • Keep the door open
What We Value

Four commitments
written on the wall.

Tap a value to read the longer version.

We taste every roast before it reaches the bar and re-dial the grinder twice a day. Craft, for us, is not perfection — it is attention, repeated.

Every guest gets the same welcome — the regular who comes daily and the visitor who wandered in by accident. There is no VIP room here, and there never will be.

Coffee is an endless subject. We cup weekly, we travel to origin when we can, and we are not embarrassed to admit we still learn something new every month.

We host a monthly cupping, keep a shelf of borrowed books, and know most of our regulars by name. A cafe is only as good as the people who choose to sit in it.

The Room

Four corners
worth knowing.

Six stools, one bar, endless opinions.

Sit here if you want to watch your coffee being made and hear the grinder argue with itself.

Morning light, west-facing, best before 10am.

Two tables by the glass. Popular with writers, readers, and people avoiding their inbox.

The quiet room. Phones on silent, please.

Deep chairs, a low ceiling, and enough distance from the grinder to actually hear yourself think.

Eight tables outside, weather permitting.

Shaded by an old fig tree in summer. Blankets provided when it isn't.
